Make Technology Learning Simple and Accessible

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about using this demo website.

Is AI LearnHub free to use?

This demo website is free to open. Some third-party course websites may have their own paid plans.

Do I need an account to browse resources?

No. You can browse learning resources without signing in. Login and sign-up are demonstration features.

How do I save a course?

Click the Save button on a course card. It will appear in the My Learning Progress section on the same browser.

Does the website store information on a server?

No. This standalone demo stores its demo account, bookmarks and progress in your browser only. It is not a production authentication system.

How does the contact form work?

It opens your email application with the message prepared. Replace the sample email address with your own email address in the website file.
